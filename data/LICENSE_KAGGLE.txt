Kaggle licence note for data/kaggle_weekly_29.csv and data/kaggle_asset_metadata.csv
====================================================================================

Dataset:  "Huge Stock Market Dataset" - Historical daily prices and volumes of all U.S. stocks and ETFs
Owner:    Boris Marjanovic (Kaggle user borismarjanovic)
URL:      https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/borismarjanovic/price-volume-data-for-all-us-stocks-etfs
Licence:  CC0: Public Domain, as reported by Kaggle's dataset metadata (licenseName field of
          https://www.kaggle.com/api/v1/datasets/view/borismarjanovic/price-volume-data-for-all-us-stocks-etfs),
          checked 2026-10-10. Dataset last updated on Kaggle 2017-11-16.

Author's statement, quoted from the dataset description:
  "Note that prices have been adjusted for dividends and splits."
  "This dataset belongs to me. I’m sharing it here for free. You may do with it as you wish."

Caveats (not resolved by us):
- The description does not name an upstream data source for the prices. We have not verified who
  originally produced them, and we rely on Kaggle's CC0 label and the author's statement above.
- No further legal review was done.

What we ship (team ZollerQu):
- kaggle_weekly_29.csv: weekly (Friday) closing prices of 29 assets + SPY, 2006-05-26 to 2017-11-10,
  derived from the "Close" column of the files ETFs/<ticker>.us.txt and Stocks/<ticker>.us.txt by
  stocks/kaggle_data.py (last close of each week). No values were invented or altered.
- kaggle_asset_metadata.csv: company/fund names and groups, added by us from the ticker symbols
  (the dataset itself has tickers only).
