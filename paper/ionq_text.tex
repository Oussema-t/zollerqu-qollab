We ran the 12-asset mode-B circuit with the highest noiseless and estimated noisy success, CVaR$_{0.1}$-QAOA at $p=3$ (198 two-qubit gates), once on the IonQ Forte~1 noise model through Qollab (1\,000 shots, 11 October 2026). 7 shots were the exact optimum (7 per 1\,000; 95\,\% interval 3--14), against 58 per 1\,000 in the noiseless simulation of the same circuit and 0.24 per 1\,000 for uniform guessing: 29$\times$ random, but 8$\times$ below noiseless. 25.1\,\% of the shots were within 5\,\% of the optimum (noiseless: 63.6\,\%). This is a single job on a noise model, not hardware; the interval covers sampling error only.
